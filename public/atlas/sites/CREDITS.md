Allowlist matches `public/atlas/parasites/CREDITS.md`: CDC DPDx / PHIL (public domain, often via Wikimedia Commons); Wikimedia Commons CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA; US government PD libraries. Anatomography / BodyParts3D renders are allowed under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP when credited to DBCLS / BodyParts3D.

Human Anatomography plates may stand for a shared site (liver, lungs, stomach, and similar). The Heroes row says so. Veterinary-only sites can stay on the existing tile until a licence-ok plate exists.

Replace-only policy: mapped keys keep a real webp. A licence-ok single-organ plate overwrites that key's file. Keys without a found plate stay on the current AI webp (listed under Still AI). Do not delete a mapped file to force a letter-only tile.
