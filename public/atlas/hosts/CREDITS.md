# Host portrait image credits

Open-licensed glance portraits under this folder.
Allowed sources: CDC DPDx / PHIL (public domain, often via Wikimedia Commons); Wikimedia Commons (CC0 / public domain / CC BY / CC BY-SA); US government public domain image libraries when the page states public domain or US Gov work (USDA ARS, CDC PHIL direct, NIH/NLM when marked PD); Figshare and Zenodo deposits whose item licence is explicitly CC0 or CC BY (BY-SA only when the deposit states BY-SA); Anatomography / BodyParts3D renders (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP; attribute DBCLS / BodyParts3D).
Rejected: AI-generated placeholders, all-rights-reserved, unclear, NC/ND, iNaturalist, Health Icons runtime deps, Iconify CDN.

Photos only. Empty until a licence-ok photo ships; no silhouette fill.
