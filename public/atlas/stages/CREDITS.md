# Atlas stage glance portrait credits

Open-licensed stage archetypes under `public/atlas/stages/`.
Allowlist matches `public/atlas/parasites/CREDITS.md`: CDC DPDx / PHIL (public domain, often via Wikimedia Commons); Wikimedia Commons CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA; US government PD libraries.

Each Heroes row names the **exemplar taxon** shown; the filename is the glance **archetype** key (honest: one species plate stands for the stage vocabulary term).

Replace-only policy: mapped keys keep a real webp. Licence-ok plates overwrite AI tiles; keys without a found plate stay on AI until a later hunt (listed under Still AI). Do not delete a mapped file to force a Missing gap.
